An unobserved value is carried as unobserved, never as zero. Every number on this page is either a FACT read from a pinned public file or a RANGE reconstructed with its lineage attached.

  1. Pin the inputs. Nine public tables are stamped and SHA256-verified into one snapshot. A live run refuses stamps that differ from the pins.
  2. Score every binding flowgate. 2,072 elements from SPP's 2025 day-ahead binding-constraint archives roll up to 892 corridors.
  3. Forecast rent. 2025 dollars × share of months binding × a persistence ratio from a held-out-months backtest (66% of held-out dollars captured, against 64% for raw annual).
  4. Decide under uncertainty. For each flowgate: find which GETs apply, then deploy the one with the highest expected net value, or none. Relief, haircut, cost and next year's congestion are drawn 10,000 times from cited priors. Applicability is set per element class, so flowgates of one class differ only in their congestion.
  5. Gate the export. Lineage that touches synthetic or non-publishable data blocks a shareable export.
  6. Replay. headroom reproduce rebuilds the ranking, the export decision and byte-identical outputs from the manifest.

What this is

SPP's day-ahead market posted $2.4B of congestion on its bottleneck elements in 2025. Grid-enhancing technologies like dynamic line rating can relieve some of it for a fraction of the cost of new lines, but where they would pay off is rarely worked out from public data.

Headroom answers that from public data only. It ranks every flowgate that bound in SPP's 2025 market by the expected net value of a GET decision, keeps the uncertainty in the answer, and refuses to publish anything it cannot trace to a public source.

What it does not know

  • Which GET fits a particular flowgate. Until location, length and loading are observed, the pick is the default for its element class.
  • Where SPP's monitored elements are. The bus map is CEII, so loading, demand pressure and radial stay unobserved.
  • SPP's planning projects. The ITP is published as PDF only.
  • Real-time congestion or a second market year. Forward rent is calibrated within 2025.
